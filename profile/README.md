# KI.SH – Artificial Intelligence Schleswig-Holstein

**Bringing AI from research into practice.**

</div>

KI.SH (*Künstliche Intelligenz Schleswig-Holstein*) is an open network connecting research institutions, universities, and businesses in Schleswig-Holstein, Germany.

Our mission is to make artificial intelligence accessible and applicable, particularly for small and medium-sized enterprises in Schleswig-Holstein, Germany. We support organizations in identifying AI opportunities, developing innovative solutions, and translating research into real-world applications.

Through collaboration between academia and industry, we promote knowledge transfer, innovation, and the practical adoption of AI technologies.

## Our Open-Source Projects

This GitHub organization hosts public repositories containing software, tools, prototypes, and research projects developed by **KI.SH**.

Explore our repositories to discover our work and learn more about the AI solutions we develop.
